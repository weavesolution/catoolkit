Privacy Policy

CA Financial Toolkit · Effective 31 October 2026

Summary

CA Financial Toolkit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or financial data. All calculations run entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not receive the figures you enter or the results produced.

Information stored on your device

  • Inputs you are working on, calculations you choose to save to History, and your preferences (firm name, theme) are stored in your device's local app storage only.
  • You can delete saved calculations at any time from History → Clear all, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Backups, PDF and Excel files you export are saved or shared only where you choose. Their handling is then governed by the destination you select (for example, your file manager or a messaging app).

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone or SMS. Network access is used only to load and update the app itself.

Children

The app is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device through the app, its security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption). Keep your device secure and be careful when sharing exported reports.

Disclaimer

Results are calculation aids based solely on the figures you enter. They are not financial, tax, legal or credit advice. Bank and regulatory norms shown are indicative. The app is not affiliated with ICAI, RBI or any bank.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
